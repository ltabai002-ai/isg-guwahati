# ISG: Trust & Growth

You are an expert web designer and front-end developer. Build a complete, modern, emotionally persuasive marketing website for a premium international school. I have attached a reference image (a full-page screenshot of a school website whose design system and structure I want you to emulate) and a link to the client's outdated old site for context: https://cambridgeinternationalschoolguwahati.com/

Study the attached image carefully. Emulate its design DNA: alternating full-bleed colour sections, one big idea per section, generous whitespace, large sentence-style headlines written in a parent's voice (not corporate labels), real-photography-first layouts, big honest animated numbers, a scrolling ticker strip, and small interactive widgets (a selector and a values explorer). Do NOT copy its green/teal/pink colours or its exact text — I'll give you a different brand palette and real content below. Study the old site only to understand the school's substance; do not reuse its dated flat layout, its generic copy, or its broken "0%" counters.

THE SINGLE MOST IMPORTANT GOAL: Every design and copy decision must make a visiting parent feel "this is the best school for my child." Lead with warmth, proof, and reassurance. Prioritise emotional connection and trust over feature-listing.

CORE POSITIONING (use this as the emotional thread): This school is the established, proven, results-backed choice — the region's first and oldest Cambridge school — not a trendy newcomer. The brand promise is the motto: "Education with pleasure, not pressure." Anchor the whole site in that line.

SCHOOL DATA (use exactly this, invent nothing else):

Name: International School Guwahati (ISG). Founded 2010 by NR Education Foundation.

Address: Nalapara, Kalimandir Path, Sarusajai P.O Saukuchi, Guwahati-781040, Assam.

Motto: "Education with Pleasure, Not Pressure."

Core values: Reflective, Innovative, Confident, Engaged, Responsible.

Curriculum: Cambridge, leading to IGCSE, AS & A Levels.

Programs: Cambridge Early Years (ages 3–6, play-based); Cambridge Primary (5–11, foundational skills across up to 10 subjects); Cambridge Lower Secondary (11–14); Cambridge Upper Secondary / IGCSE (14–16, 70+ subjects); Cambridge Advanced / AS & A Level (16–19, pre-university specialisation).

Class size: max 20 students per section up to IGCSE; 12–15 for AS & A Level.

Accreditation: Cambridge International Education (CIE).

Results (frame exactly like this): "63% A*–B at IGCSE" (do not show a raw breakdown).

University placement: 80% Europe, 10% North America (USA), 7% India, 3% Asia. Headline it as "90% of graduates go on to universities abroad."

Faculty: 45+ teachers; 14% hold a PhD, 58% hold a Master's.

Standout numbers: 15+ years, KG–A Level, max 20 per class, 10+ nationalities.

Facilities: science labs, library, swimming pool, sports room, theatre.

Admissions steps: Enquiry → Campus tour → Assessment → Interview → Enrolment.

Fees: not shown publicly — "shared on request." Sibling discounts available.

Academic year starts in April; admissions open in October.

Alumni community exists; feature it lightly.

BRAND SYSTEM:

Colours: deep Blue (primary), Maroon (accent, for CTAs and highlights), White/off-white (space). This palette must feel classic, trusted and institutional — deliberately different from the reference image. Use blue for structure, maroon sparingly for calls to action and key numbers.

Typography: a warm, high-legibility serif for headlines (trust, heritage) paired with a clean sans-serif for body. Headlines large and confident.

Tone of every headline: a sentence spoken to a parent, e.g. "Fifteen years of watching children grow into themselves," not "About Us."

Use tasteful scroll-reveal animations, hover states, and count-up number animations. Smooth, premium, never gimmicky.

Fully responsive, mobile-first, fast, accessible (good contrast, alt text, keyboard-navigable).

BUILD THESE SECTIONS IN THIS ORDER:

Sticky header: logo placeholder (text "International School Guwahati"), nav (About, The Cambridge Pathway, School Life, Admissions, News & Events, Contact), and a maroon "Book a Visit" button. Add a floating WhatsApp button on all pages.

Hero: full-bleed real-student photo (use a warm placeholder). Headline: "Education with pleasure, not pressure." Subline: "North East's first Cambridge school. Fifteen years, from age 3 to A Level — and graduates now studying across Europe, the USA and beyond." Two buttons: "Book a campus visit" (maroon) and "Explore the Cambridge pathway." A thin stat strip under it: Est. 2010 · KG–A Level · Max 20 per class · 45+ teachers.

Scrolling ticker strip (marquee): "Cambridge Early Years to A Level ◆ Max 20 students per class ◆ 90% of graduates to universities abroad ◆ First Cambridge school in the North East ◆" repeating.

Interactive "Choose your child's stage" selector: five tappable tabs (Early Years 3–6, Primary 5–11, Lower Secondary 11–14, Upper Secondary/IGCSE 14–16, Advanced/AS & A Level 16–19). Selecting one reveals a panel with tailored copy: what a child does at that stage, the class size, and where it leads. Warm, reassuring, parent-facing.

"A day at ISG" timeline: an hour-by-hour school day (arrival & check-in, small-group lessons, science lab, break/swimming, theatre or art, home), each row paired with a photo and one line on what that hour gives the child. This turns facilities into lived experience.

Honest stats band (animated count-up, four numbers only): 15+ years · 63% A*–B at IGCSE · 90% of graduates to universities abroad · max 20 per class. Never render empty or "0%" values.

Core values interactive explorer (like the reference's competency widget): five items — Reflective, Innovative, Confident, Engaged, Responsible — tap each to reveal a one-line meaning and how the school nurtures it.

"Why families choose the established school" section — the emotional trust anchor. Region's first and oldest Cambridge school, 15 years of proven results, senior faculty (14% PhD, 58% Master's), alumni across four continents. Confident, reassuring tone.

University outcomes: a simple world-map or stat visualisation — 80% Europe, 10% North America, 7% India, 3% Asia — framed as proof the approach works.

Testimonials: four short pull-quote cards with name + role (use realistic placeholders like "Parent of a Grade 10 student," "Alumnus, now at university in the USA"). Not long paragraphs.

Leadership: one warm message card from the Chairman, with the line "The aim of education should be to teach the child to think, not what to think."

Admissions journey: a horizontal 5-step visual stepper (Enquiry → Campus tour → Assessment → Interview → Enrolment). Note: academic year starts April, admissions open October, sibling discounts available, fees shared on request.

Enquiry / book-a-visit section: a clean form (child's entering grade, parent name, phone, email, message) with reassuring copy: "Tell us about your child. We'll call you back the same day."

Footer: real address with Google Maps embed, phone, email, office hours, quick links, and a newsletter sign-up. Leave clean space for social icons (none yet). No fake social links.

TECHNICAL & FUTURE-PROOFING:

Include a News & Events / blog section and a photo gallery page (grid with lightbox).

Structure the codebase and navigation so a gated parent login portal can be added later without a rebuild.

English only.

Use high-quality warm placeholder images throughout with descriptive alt text; I'll swap in real photos later.

Every CTA should route to the enquiry form or the book-a-visit flow.

Build the full multi-section homepage first with all the copy written out (no lorem ipsum — use the real, parent-facing copy in the spirit above), then stub the inner pages (About, Pathway, School Life, Admissions, News & Events, Contact). Make it feel premium, warm, trustworthy, and unmistakably "the best school for my child."
